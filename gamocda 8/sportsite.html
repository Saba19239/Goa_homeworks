about football

football is a sport played by 2 teams, consisting of 11 players, the goal of the game is to outscore the opponent.

the rules

a normal match lasts 90 minutes, divided into two 45 minute halves, outfield players cannot use their hands to interact with the ball (outside of some special circumstences) the only person allowed to touch the ball is the goalkeeper if they are in their own penalty box.
