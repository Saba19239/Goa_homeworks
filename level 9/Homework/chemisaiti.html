LAMBORGHINI FUN FACTS

Founded in 1963 by tractor tycoon Ferruccio Lamborghini to rival Ferrari, Automobili Lamborghini began as a challenge to create the perfect grand tourer. Known for naming cars after bullfighting, featuring iconic scissor doors, and using powerful V12 engines, the company is now part of the Volkswagen Group via Audi AG
