⚔️ The Fall of Constantinople (1453)

One of the most dramatic turning points in history is the fall of Constantinople, when the city of Constantinople was captured by the Ottoman Empire in 1453.

In 1453, a 21-year-old sultan named Mehmed II launched a massive siege on Constantinople, the capital of the Byzantine Empire. The city had massive walls that had protected it for over 1,000 years, Mehmed used giant cannons “Basilica” (also known as the Great Turkish Bombard). to break them, the siege lasted about 7 weeks

After the Fall of Constantinople, the Ottoman Empire took control of key land routes between Europe and Asia. So Europeans started looking for new sea routes: Portugal sailed around Africa toward India Spain tried going west across the ocean In 1492, Christopher Columbus, sailing for Spain, crossed the Atlantic and reached the Americas thinking he had found a new route to Asia. This kicked off the Age of Exploration Europe and the Americas became connected for the first time It completely changed world history (trade, empires, cultures)
