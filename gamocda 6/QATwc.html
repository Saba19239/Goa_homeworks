World Cup 2022

The 2022 World Cup in Qatar was one of the most dramatic tournaments in history. Argentina won the trophy after defeating France on penalties in a legendary 3-3 final, many recognise this as the greatest world cup in history. One of the biggest upsets was Saudi Arabia defeating Argentina 2-1 in the group stage, the biggest shock was Morocco, by becoming the first African and Arab team to reach the semi-finals.

Winner: Argentina

Runner-up: France

Shock: Morocco

Dissapoint: Spain
