World Cup 2018

The 2018 World Cup in Russia was full of surprises. France won their second World Cup after defeating Croatia 4-2 in Croatia's first ever World Cup final. Mexico shocked defending champions Germany in the group stage, while South Korea delivered one of the tournament's biggest shocks by beating Germany 2-0 and eliminating them in the group stage.
